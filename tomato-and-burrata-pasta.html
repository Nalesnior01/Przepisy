Tomato and Burrata Pasta

Serves 7 people

Ingredients

  • 300 g tomatoes
  • Olive oil
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Basil
  • Parmesan
  • Burrata

Instructions

  1. Cook the tomatoes in olive oil in a frying pan for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and cook for 5 minutes, then season with salt and pepper and cook for another 3 minutes.
  3. Blend the mixture with basil and burrata.
  4. Return the sauce to the pan, add the pasta and finish with Parmesan.
