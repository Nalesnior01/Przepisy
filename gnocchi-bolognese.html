Gnocchi “Bolognese”

Serves 4 people

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef
  • 600 g gnocchi
  • 600 g passata
  • 200 g carrots
  • 1 large onion
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • Chilli pepper
  • 100 g mozzarella
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Oregano

Instructions

  1. Place the minced beef, gnocchi, passata, chopped carrots, onion, garlic cloves and chilli into 4 ovenproof dishes.
  2. Season with salt, pepper and oregano.
  3. Top with mozzarella and bake in an oven preheated to 180°C for 45 minutes.
