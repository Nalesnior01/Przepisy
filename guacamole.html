Guacamole

Serves 4 people

Ingredients

  • 3 avocados
  • 1 large tomato
  • Red onion
  • Lime
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Coriander

Instructions

  1. Mash the avocados in a bowl.
  2. Add the tomato, cut into small pieces, and chopped onion. Mix with the mashed avocado.
  3. Season with salt and pepper and add the juice of half a lime.
  4. Add coriander according to preference.
