Spaghetti Pomodoro with Meatballs

Serves 6 people

Ingredients

  • For the meatballs:
  • 700 g beef
  • 3 tbsp parsley
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • ½ chilli pepper
  • 80 g Parmigiano
  • 40 g Pecorino
  • 1 egg
  • Bread
  • Milk
  • Pepper
  • Salt
  • For the tomato sauce:
  • 2 cans of tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp basil
  • 2 shallots
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • White wine
  • Spaghetti, as needed

Instructions

  1. Soak the bread in milk.
  2. Mix the minced meat with the cheeses, parsley, chopped chilli and garlic.
  3. Add the soaked bread, season with salt and pepper (ideally with the pepper toasted in a pan), then add the whole egg and combine thoroughly.
  4. Form very small meatballs and fry them in hot olive oil.
  5. Set the meatballs aside. Using the remaining fat, add a little white wine, soften the shallots, then add the canned tomatoes. Season with basil and 1 tsp oregano.
  6. When the whole tomatoes have broken down, add the cooked pasta to the sauce and mix well.
  7. Finally, add the meatballs and serve with Parmigiano.
