Pesto with Basil, Parsley or Coriander

Serves x people

Ingredients

  • Basil, parsley or coriander
  • 100 g Grana Padano
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • Olive oil
  • A handful of pine nuts or flaked almonds

Instructions

  1. Chop the garlic and toast the pine nuts or flaked almonds.
  2. Add the cheese and basil, parsley or coriander.
  3. Blend everything together, controlling the consistency with olive oil.
