Paella

Serves 5 people

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken
  • 800 g paella rice
  • 200 g carrots
  • 1 can of kidney beans
  • 1 can of peas
  • 1 can of tomatoes
  • 1 large onion
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 2 litres vegetable stock
  • Red pepper
  • Lemon
  • Sweet paprika
  • Cumin
  • Turmeric

Instructions

  1. Coat the chicken with the spices and squeeze over half a lemon, then fry.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large pan and add the chopped onion. Allow it to colour, then add the garlic, carrot and pepper and cook until softened.
  3. Add the chicken, pour in the stock and add the uncooked rice.
  4. Once the rice has absorbed the liquid and swollen, add the kidney beans and peas.
  5. Squeeze over lemon juice and add lemon slices.
