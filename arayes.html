Arayes

Serves 2–3 people

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 100 g parsley
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 1 tsp garlic
  • 1 tsp cardamom
  • 1 tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp chilli
  • 1 tbsp pepper
  • ½ tbsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • For the sauce:
  • 200 g Greek yoghurt
  • 50 g parsley
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Lemon zest
  • Pitas

Instructions

  1. For the filling: mix the beef, chopped garlic, onion and parsley together. Add the chopped jalapeño and spices. Mix well and leave covered in the fridge for at least 1 hour or overnight.
  2. For the sauce: add grated garlic and parsley to the yoghurt, then add the spices and olive oil. Mix thoroughly and add lemon zest to taste.
  3. Cut the pitas in half and fill them with the meat mixture.
  4. Sear them on a very hot grill pan for 1 minute on each side.
  5. Transfer them to an oven preheated to 180°C for about 10 minutes, until the meat is fully cooked and the pitas are crispy.
