Chilli Con Carne

Serves 4 people

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef
  • 150 g carrots
  • 400 g rice
  • 300 ml vegetable stock
  • 250 g passata
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 large onion
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 1 can of tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 can of kidney beans
  • 1 can of sweetcorn
  • 1 jar of olives
  • Chilli flakes
  • Cumin
  • Sweet paprika
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Add the chopped onion to hot olive oil in a large pan.
  2. After 5 minutes, add the garlic, then the carrots and pepper.
  3. After a moment, add all the spices and tomato purée. Stir well for another 5 minutes until the carrot and pepper soften slightly.
  4. Increase the heat and add the beef. Once it has browned, reduce the heat and add the vegetable stock.
  5. Once the liquid has evaporated, add the canned tomatoes and passata.
  6. Cook over low heat for some time. Finally, add the kidney beans, sweetcorn and olives.
  7. Serve with rice.
