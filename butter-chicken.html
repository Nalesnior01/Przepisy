Butter Chicken

Serves 6 people

Ingredients

  • 250 g chicken breast (or more)
  • 2 cans of tomatoes
  • 2 red onions
  • 4–5 cloves of garlic
  • 3 tbsp Greek yoghurt or 12% cream, to taste
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 5 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp chilli
  • For the chicken marinade:
  • 2 tbsp yoghurt
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp coriander
  • ½ tsp chilli
  • Pitas, to serve

Instructions

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces. Add the spices and yoghurt for the marinade, mix and leave in the fridge for at least 1 hour.
  2. Slice the onions and garlic. Heat olive oil in a pan and soften the onion and garlic.
  3. Add the bay leaves and canned tomatoes. Rinse the cans with water and add some of this water to the sauce.
  4. Cover and cook over medium heat until the tomatoes soften.
  5. Remove the bay leaves, add the spices and blend the sauce.
  6. Leave over low heat and add the yoghurt or cream.
  7. Place the marinated chicken in an oven preheated to 200°C for about 15–20 minutes.
  8. Add the chicken to the sauce, season to taste and serve with warm pitas.
