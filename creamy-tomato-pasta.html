Creamy Tomato Pasta

Serves 5 people

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1½ onions
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 300 ml passata
  • 100 ml pasta water
  • 200 ml cream
  • 100 g cheese
  • 30 g butter
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Chilli flakes
  • Parmesan, to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat 4 tbsp olive oil in a pan and add 1½ onions and 4 cloves of garlic. Cook until softened.
  2. Add 5 tbsp of tomatoes and cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Add 100 ml pasta water, or more if needed, to achieve the desired consistency.
  4. Once the sauce comes to the boil, add 100 g cheese, 2 tbsp butter and 200 ml cream. Remove from the heat.
  5. Season with salt, pepper and chilli flakes. Serve with Parmesan.
