Spaghetti Aglio e Olio with Toppings

Serves 5 people

Ingredients

  • 500 g pasta
  • 300 g pancetta
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • Plenty of olive oil
  • Fresh parsley
  • Peperoncino or freshly ground toasted pepper, to taste
  • Parmesan
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Place the pancetta, cut into 1 cm × 1 cm cubes, in a cold frying pan.
  2. Once crispy, drain the rendered fat into a bowl and transfer the pancetta to another container.
  3. While the pasta is cooking, gently fry sliced garlic and peperoncino or freshly ground toasted pepper in olive oil over low heat, allowing them to brown without burning.
  4. When the garlic becomes golden, add a ladleful of pasta water to stop the cooking and set the pan aside.
  5. Drain the pasta very al dente, transfer it to the pan with the olive oil and season.
  6. Garnish with plenty of chopped parsley and Parmesan. Serve hot with the crispy pancetta.
