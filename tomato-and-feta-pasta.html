Tomato and Feta Pasta

Serves x people

Ingredients

  • 900 g cherry tomatoes
  • 225 g feta cheese (one block)
  • 120 ml olive oil
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • ¼ cup fresh basil, chopped, plus extra to serve
  • Rosemary

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Place the cherry tomatoes in an ovenproof dish.
  2. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Mix well.
  3. Place the block of feta in the centre of the dish, surrounded by the tomatoes. Turn the cheese a few times so it is coated with the oil and seasoning.
  4. Add the two whole garlic cloves and rosemary. Bake for 35 minutes, until the tomatoes start to burst and the feta melts.
  5. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in salted water according to the packet instructions until al dente. Drain, reserving 120 ml of pasta water.
  6. Remove the tomatoes from the oven and remove the rosemary. Mash and mix everything else together. Add the pasta to the sauce and mix thoroughly.
