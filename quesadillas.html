Quesadillas

Serves 4 people

Ingredients

  • Guacamole
  • For the filling:
  • 500 g beef or chicken
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • Lime
  • Orange
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • Fresh coriander
  • For the salsa:
  • 400 g tomatillos
  • White onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • Jalapeño
  • Lime
  • Fresh coriander
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Oregano
  • Avocado
  • Olive oil
  • Small tortillas
  • Cheddar

Instructions

  1. Cut the meat into small pieces. Add all the spices, 1 tsp olive oil, the juice of half a lime and half an orange, and chopped coriander and garlic.
  2. Mix well and leave in the fridge for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, blanch the tomatillo skins, quarter the onion and halve the jalapeño.
  4. Fry the tomatillos, jalapeño and onion together with two whole cloves of garlic.
  5. When the onion has browned, add a little boiling water to the pan and transfer everything to a blender.
  6. Add the juice of half a lime, the whole avocado and fresh coriander. Season with salt, pepper and oregano, then blend until smooth.
  7. Fry the meat in a pan.
  8. Heat the tortillas and sprinkle with cheddar until it melts. Add the cooked meat and fold the tortilla in half to finish cooking.
  9. Serve with the prepared salsa and guacamole.
