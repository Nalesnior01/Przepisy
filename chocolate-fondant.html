Chocolate Fondant

Serves 2 people

Ingredients

  • 100 g dark chocolate
  • 50 g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 40 g sugar
  • 30 g wheat flour
  • Icing sugar
  • Ice cream
  • Fruit

Instructions

  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter.
  2. Lightly beat the eggs, add the sugar and mix well.
  3. Combine the two mixtures and mix again, adding the flour.
  4. Pour the mixture into moulds and bake at 200°C for about 8–9 minutes. Finding the perfect timing requires some trial and error.
  5. Place the fondant on a plate already dusted with icing sugar. Dust with icing sugar again and serve with fruit and a scoop of ice cream.
