Bolognese Pasta

Serves 6 people

Ingredients

  • 700 g beef
  • 100 g carrots
  • 100 g celery
  • 100 g onion
  • 800 g tomatoes
  • 250 ml red wine
  • 250 ml full-fat milk
  • Olive oil + a little butter
  • Rosemary

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a frying pan and add chopped rosemary.
  2. Add butter, carrot, onion and celery. Allow them to soften.
  3. Add half of the wine and cook until the liquid evaporates.
  4. Add the beef, break it up, and pour in the remaining wine. Cook until the liquid evaporates.
  5. Add the tomatoes and cook over low heat for a long time. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Add the milk after some time and continue cooking for a long time.
  7. Serve with Parmesan.
