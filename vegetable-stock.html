Vegetable Stock

Makes 2 litres

Ingredients

  • 3 carrots
  • 1 onion
  • 1 parsnip root
  • ½ bunch of dill
  • 3 celery stalks
  • Parsley leaves
  • ½ head of garlic
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3 allspice berries
  • 1 tsp peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp salt + more to taste
  • 2 litres water

Instructions

  1. Peel all the ingredients lightly, wash them well and place them in a pot.
  2. Cover with water and bring to the boil over high heat.
  3. Then simmer over low heat for 2 hours and add salt to taste.
  4. Strain through a sieve and discard the vegetables.
