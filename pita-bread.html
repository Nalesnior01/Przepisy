Pita Bread

Serves 2 people

Ingredients

  • 500 g wheat flour
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 packet instant dry yeast
  • 330 ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. Add the yeast to the warm water and mix well.
  2. Add the flour, salt and olive oil. Knead the dough and leave it to rise for about 1.5 hours.
  3. Divide the dough into smaller balls and let them rest under a warm cloth.
  4. Roll them out and cook in a frying pan (flat) or in the oven (puffed) until browned.
  5. Keep the finished pitas in a bowl covered with a warm cloth.
