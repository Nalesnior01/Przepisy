Creamy Salmon Pasta

Serves 2 very hungry people or 3 less hungry people

Ingredients

  • 1 small onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 150 g smoked salmon
  • 1 lemon
  • ¾ of a pack of 30% cream
  • Dill

Instructions

  1. Bring the pasta water to the boil. Dice the onion and slice the garlic. Add the pasta to salted boiling water.
  2. Heat olive oil in a frying pan and add the onion. Once it starts to brown, add the garlic.
  3. Add the chopped salmon and lemon zest.
  4. Finally, add the cream and some pasta water until the sauce reaches the desired consistency. Stir all the ingredients together over low heat.
  5. Add the pasta to the sauce and sprinkle with dill.
