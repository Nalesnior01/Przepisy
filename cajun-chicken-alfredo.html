Cajun Chicken Alfredo

Serves 5 people

Ingredients

  • For the chicken:
  • 2 packs of chicken breasts
  • 2 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp ground garlic
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp basil
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • ½ tsp cayenne pepper
  • For the sauce:
  • Parmesan
  • 200 ml 30% cream
  • White wine
  • Parsley
  • 5 large cloves of garlic
  • Butter
  • 500 g pasta
  • Olive oil

Instructions

  1. Mix all the spices together.
  2. Coat the chicken breasts with olive oil in a bowl, then coat them with the spice mixture.
  3. Fry the chicken in a hot pan with olive oil. Once cooked, set it aside.
  4. Pour a little wine into the remaining pan juices, add crushed garlic and a piece of butter.
  5. After a moment, add the cream and stir well.
  6. Add grated cheese and, once melted, add the chicken to the sauce.
  7. Mix the cooked pasta into the sauce with the chicken cut into slices. Serve with Parmesan and parsley.
