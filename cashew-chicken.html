Cashew Chicken

Serves 10 people

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken breast
  • 4 packs of rice
  • 4 onions
  • ½ head of garlic
  • 1½ red peppers
  • 1½ green peppers
  • 1½ yellow peppers
  • ½ chilli pepper
  • 1 can of sweetcorn
  • 1 can of pineapple
  • 2 packs of cashew nuts
  • Ginger
  • Spring onion
  • For the sauce:
  • 6 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Toast the pepper and cashew nuts over low heat.
  2. Mix all the sauce ingredients in a bowl and add the ground toasted pepper.
  3. Add 4 tbsp of the sauce to the chopped chicken, wrap it in cling film and leave it in the fridge for 15 minutes.
  4. Start cooking the rice.
  5. Add chopped garlic and ginger and quartered onion to hot olive oil in a wok. Stir until browned.
  6. Add the peppers and, once softened, add the sweetcorn and pineapple.
  7. Fry the chicken separately until browned.
  8. Add the chicken to the wok and pour in the remaining sauce. Cook until hot.
  9. Serve with rice and garnish with chopped spring onion and cashew nuts crushed into large pieces.
